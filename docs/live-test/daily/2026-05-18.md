Investigation traced the issue to APScheduler's `CronTrigger.from_crontab` not remapping day-of-week numbering: the standard crontab `1-5` (Mon–Fri) is being passed through to APScheduler's internal `CronTrigger` where the day_of_week field uses 0=Mon, so `1-5` = Tue–Sat. The logged trigger string `cron[..., day_of_week='1-5', ...]` and the `next run at: 2026-05-19 18:00:00 +07` (Tuesday) line after Saturday's execution both confirm this. The previous claim in the May 13–15 logs that "the cron is now armed for Mon–Fri" was incorrect — it has actually been Tue–Sat all along, which is why the cron also fired on Saturday May 16 (when markets are closed and the refresh writes a no-change snapshot).

(b) Change the env-var default from `0 18 * * 1-5` to `0 18 * * 0-4` so it lands on Mon–Fri under APScheduler's numbering.
