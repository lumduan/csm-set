## Executive Summary

June was the live test's second month and its first rebalance month. It opened with the **June 2 ATO rebalance** (the Option C backtest top-10: sold NEX/AGE/JTS, bought KCE/EASTW/EPG), completed the MCOT leg in two tranches (Jun 4–5), and absorbed a **100,000 THB capital injection** on June 4 — the live-test capital base was rebased 1,000,000 → 1,100,000 in lockstep so the injection is not booked as a return, and the equity series was restarted at the Jun 5 baseline of 1,095,967.18. From that baseline NAV traced a deep V: a first trough of 1,053,006.18 on Jun 10 (−3.92%) and a second, deeper drawdown to 1,057,233.18 on Jun 23 — the month's **−4.35% maximum drawdown**, measured from the Jun 22 peak of 1,105,317.18 — before a strong four-session rally into month-end carried the book to a fresh all-time high of **1,119,968.18** on Jun 30, eclipsing the prior Jun 4 peak (1,119,768.31) by +199.87 THB.

Dispersion stayed wide. **GUNKUL (+44.98% U.PL at month-end)** was the standout leader, **INSET (+20.59%)** the second, and a late-month electronics/water surge (KCE, EASTW, HANA) drove the final-week rally. The persistent deep laggards were **IRPC (−20.56%)**, **PTTGC (−18.43%)** and **MCOT (−15.53%)**. Regime was **BULL every session** (SET held a +13–16% cushion over its SMA200), so the 99% deployment ran unconstrained and the −10% circuit breaker never came within ~5.6 pp of tripping. The annualized Sharpe on the restart series was **1.15** (small sample, n=17). The month's defining methodology point is that the July rebalance is the **first fully systematic one** of the live test — see the Rebalance section.

## Rebalance (July 1 ATO)

Month-end evaluation ran against a **re-fetch of the dividend-adjusted history through Jun 30 incl. the `SET:SET` index**, so the authoritative composite is computable (the daily refresh banks only the four momentum factors — see Notes). **July 1 (Wed) is the next trading session** and the ATO execution day (operator to confirm no SET holiday).

This is the live test's **first fully systematic rebalance**: all three tested exit rules plus the liquidity screen are applied to the current book, and the result is a clean **1-out / 1-in** rotation that needs no discretionary override.
