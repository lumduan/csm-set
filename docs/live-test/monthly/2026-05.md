# Monthly Review — 2026-05 (May)

**Period:** May 2026 (live-test month 1, 19 trading days, May 5–29)
**Phase:** A — Setup & Calibration
**Status:** Holding 9 positions into the June 2 ATO rebalance; NAV closed +0.33% above the 1,000,000 THB starting capital

---

## Executive Summary

The first full month of the CSM-SET live test ran 19 trading sessions from the May 5 entry to the May 29 month-end plan day, with **no trades between entry and month-end** — the strategy held all 9 positions exactly as the backtest-aligned design intends (exits only at the monthly rebalance). NAV traced a deep V: it drifted under water through mid-month to a live-test trough of **953,987.71 THB (−4.60%) on May 19**, then recovered across the back half to a peak of **1,024,158.71 THB on May 27**, before a sharp −2.00% final session (May 29, the worst single day of the month) left the book at **1,003,342.71 THB, +0.33% cumulative**. The month therefore closed *positive* and above the 1,000,000 line — the first close above starting capital that held into a month-end.

Performance was driven by wide single-name dispersion: **GUNKUL (+36.09% U.PL at month-end)** was the standout, clearing the 4.00 round number, while **IRPC (−21.00%)** and **PTTGC (−18.43%)** broke to fresh lows on the final session and anchor the rebalance exit slate. Regime was **BULL every session** (SET held a +13–16% cushion over SMA200), so the 96% deployment ran unconstrained and the −10% circuit breaker never came close to tripping. The defining operational event of the month was a **scheduler cron day-of-week bug** (fired Tue–Sat instead of Mon–Fri) surfaced May 18 and fixed/validated by May 21 — see `events/2026-05-18-scheduler-cron-misfire.md`.
