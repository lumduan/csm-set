## Executive Summary

July opened on the MCOT→FORTH rotation executed at the July 1 ATO and immediately gave ground — NAV fell to a month-low **1,105,957.35 on July 2**, −1.25% below the June 30 baseline — before a ten-session advance that ran from July 9 to July 22 and set fresh all-time highs on the last ten of them, peaking at **1,262,400.35 on 2026-07-22**. The final seven sessions gave back −35,660.00 THB of that peak. The month's shape is therefore a long climb and a sharp, incomplete give-back: month-open **1,119,968.18** → peak **1,262,400.35** → close **1,226,740.35**, a **+9.53%** month with a **−7.11%** maximum drawdown taken entirely in the last four sessions. Thirteen of twenty-one sessions closed up (hit rate 61.9%), and the realized daily volatility was **1.98%** — but that number is dominated by two consecutive days at the very end.

Those two days are the month's defining feature and its main lesson. **2026-07-30 fell −45,063.00 THB (−3.70%)**, the largest single-day decline of the restart series and the second-largest of the live test, on a zero-gainer tape in which the electronics sleeve broke — HANA −10.07%, KCE −9.04% — and the above-cost count collapsed 7/3 → 4/6. **2026-07-31 rose +54,062.00 THB (+4.61%)**, the largest price-driven single-session gain of the live test, on 8 up / 0 down / 2 flat, with the same sleeve leading the recovery and the count returning to 7/3. A book that can move ±4% on consecutive sessions is behaving exactly as a ten-name concentrated expression of a cross-sectional momentum edge should be expected to behave, and the month's headline Sharpe should be read in that light rather than as a stable estimate. Dispersion at month-end runs from **GUNKUL +72.67%** to **DELTA −12.99%**, an **85.66 pp** spread; six names are above cost by more than 7% and two are below by more than 10%. The regime held **BULL** on all twenty-one sessions. The month's defining methodology point is that the August eviction is produced by the **trend filter, not the ranking** — DELTA still ranks in the top 9% of the universe on composite momentum and is removed solely because it closed below its 100-day EMA.
