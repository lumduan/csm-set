## Executive Summary

July opened on the MCOT→FORTH rotation executed at the July 1 ATO and immediately gave ground — NAV fell to a month-low **1,105,957.35 on July 2**, −1.25% below the June 30 baseline — before a ten-session advance that ran from July 9 to July 22 and set fresh all-time highs on the last ten of them, peaking at **1,262,400.35 on 2026-07-22**. The final seven sessions gave back −35,660.00 THB of that peak. The month's shape is therefore a long climb and a sharp, incomplete give-back: month-open **1,119,968.18** → peak **1,262,400.35** → close **1,226,740.35**, a **+9.53%** month with a **−7.11%** maximum drawdown taken entirely in the last four sessions. Thirteen of twenty-one sessions closed up (hit rate 61.9%), and the realized daily volatility was **1.98%** — but that number is dominated by two consecutive days at the very end.

Those two days are the month's defining feature and its main lesson. **2026-07-30 fell −45,063.00 THB (−3.70%)**, the largest single-day decline of the restart series and the second-largest of the live test, on a zero-gainer tape in which the electronics sleeve broke — HANA −10.07%, KCE −9.04% — and the above-cost count collapsed 7/3 → 4/6. **2026-07-31 rose +54,062.00 THB (+4.61%)**, the largest price-driven single-session gain of the live test, on 8 up / 0 down / 2 flat, with the same sleeve leading the recovery and the count returning to 7/3. A book that can move ±4% on consecutive sessions is behaving exactly as a ten-name concentrated expression of a cross-sectional momentum edge should be expected to behave, and the month's headline Sharpe should be read in that light rather than as a stable estimate. Dispersion at month-end runs from **GUNKUL +72.67%** to **DELTA −12.99%**, an **85.66 pp** spread; six names are above cost by more than 7% and two are below by more than 10%. The regime held **BULL** on all twenty-one sessions. The month's defining methodology point is that the two August evictions fire on **two different rules**: DELTA is removed by the **trend filter, not the ranking** — it still ranks in the top 13% of the universe on composite momentum and goes only because it closed below its 100-day EMA — while PTTGC is removed by the **buffer**, and only because correcting a defective universe (see Rebalance) surfaced a stronger challenger than the truncated one contained.

## Notes / Data Quality

- **Data completeness: 100% (21 / 21 trading days).** Every session produced a bar, a daily log and a DB write-back. July 28 and July 29 were market closures (`PM_HOLIDAYS`) and correctly produced no price bar.
- **Ranking-pipeline gap persists (recurring, third rebalance running).** `daily_refresh` (`api/scheduler/jobs.py`) calls `FeaturePipeline.build()` without `SET:SET` in the fetched set and without `symbol_sectors`, so `residual_momentum` / `sharpe_momentum` (index-dependent) and `sector_rel_strength` are never written — today's `features_latest.parquet` carries only the four `mom_*` factors. The authoritative six-factor composite therefore required a **manual re-fetch** of dividend-adjusted history including the index, built into a scratch store so production artifacts were untouched. This gap has now forced a manual step at the May, June and July month-ends and is the highest-value pipeline fix outstanding (follow-up #1 of `events/2026-06-30-rebalance-systematic-and-pipeline-gap.md`). It matters materially: `residual_momentum` is the only signal that passed the historical ICIR > 0.15 gate, and it is the one not being computed.
- **The universe itself was defective — corrected 2026-08-01, and it changed this month's trade list.** The live `universe_latest` held **136 symbols**; rebuilding the *same* 2026-04-30 snapshot against either the old or the refreshed raw store yields **197**. The stored snapshot was built on **2026-05-04 while `fetch_history.py` was still progressively filling the raw store** (it skips symbols already present, so the store filled over successive runs) and captured a partially filled store. The universe was therefore silently truncated to ~69% of its intended size for three months — spanning the July 1 rebalance and the originally published August plan. Regenerated end-to-end on 2026-08-01: symbol list refreshed from settfex (703 → 702; +BANPUU +TBSP / −BANPU −PTECH −SVI), the full raw store re-fetched (693 of 703 symbols; the 10 failures are `&`/`-` tickers tvkit's normalizer cannot parse, and were never in the old store either), and snapshots rebuilt for 2026-05-29 / 06-30 / 07-31. The current universe is **211 symbols**; the ranking ran on **204** of them. Historical snapshots before 2026-05-29 were deliberately **not** rebuilt: the current symbol list no longer contains delisted names (BANPU, PTECH, SVI), so rebuilding them would drop those names from past snapshots and inject **survivorship bias** into any point-in-time backtest.
- **BANPU was a ticker change, not a fetch failure.** `SET:BANPU` had been the terminal fetch miss since 2026-07-17. settfex lists no BANPU but does list **`BANPUU` — "BANPU PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED"**, the same company, and prices are continuous (5.70 on 2026-05-29 → 5.75 on 2026-07-15). Corrected in both the engine store and this universe. **`SET:BPP` is halted, not broken** — it resolves and returns bars ending 2026-07-16, and settfex's YTD close as of 07-30 is the same 12.00, i.e. SET is carrying the last price forward. BPP consequently fails the 90-bar volume/coverage screen and has dropped out of the regenerated universe, which is the correct outcome.
